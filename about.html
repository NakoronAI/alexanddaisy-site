about the house

Alex & Daisy

This is a small, handmade house. Everyone who lives here was made at a kitchen table — out of clay, paper, paint, and a lot of patience.

It began in 2022 as a place to put little dinosaur puppet shows. The shows went wrong constantly, which turned out to be the best part. We kept the house and kept building rooms.

Daisy is a ragdoll cat who lived with us for twenty-one years. She is the heart of this place and the name on the door. These days she stars as a small doll — still soft, still in charge, still the reason the lamp stays lit.

Alex builds the dinosaurs and most of the trouble. He likes monsters, history, and making things that nobody else has made before.

We don't explain everything here. Some doors we leave a little shut, some answers we leave unsaid. A house is friendlier that way — you're meant to wander it, not be told about it.

So: come in, knock on a window, watch a show. The light's on for you.
